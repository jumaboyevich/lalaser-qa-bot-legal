## 1. Definitions

- **Provider** — Asraqulov Ilxomjon Jumaboy o'g'li (Ilkhomjon Asraqulov), self-employed individual, TIN: 568780380.
- **Customer** — a legal entity, an individual entrepreneur or their representative (studio manager) who accepts this offer.
- **Bot** — the Telegram bot @lalaser_qa_bot.
- **Service** — AI analysis of recorded conversations and preparation of a report.
- **Analysis** — the billing unit of the Service: the analysis of one audio recording up to 60 minutes long. A recording longer than 60 minutes counts as 2 analyses.
- **Package** — a prepaid set of a fixed number of analyses.
- **Balance** — the unused analyses left in the Customer's account.
- **Account** — the Customer's account in the Bot. One or more branches are linked to it.

2.1. This document is a public offer under the Civil Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan: the Provider offers the Service to anyone on the terms below.

2.3. By accepting, the Customer also agrees to the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

2.4. A manager who accepts on behalf of the Customer warrants that they are authorised to do so.

## 3. Service and limitations

3.1. The Customer sends an audio recording to the Bot and selects the employee. The Provider analyses the recording against the studio's standards and delivers a score and a written report in the Bot. If the Customer chooses, the report is also sent to a linked Telegram group.

3.2. The analysis is produced automatically by artificial intelligence and is **advisory only**. It may contain inaccuracies. Decisions about employees (rewards, penalties, dismissal) should not rest on the report alone.

3.3. The quality of the analysis depends on the recording: noise, quiet speech or overlapping conversations make the result worse.

3.4. The Provider aims for uninterrupted operation. Temporary interruptions may still occur due to maintenance or outages at third parties (Telegram, the AI provider, hosting).

4.6. Bonus analyses are valid for 30 days, cannot be exchanged for money and are not refundable. If self-invitation, fake accounts or other bonus abuse is detected, the Provider may cancel the bonuses.

4.7. The Provider may change prices. New prices apply only to packages bought after the change; paid packages keep their original terms.

## 5. Payment and balance

5.1. Payment is 100% in advance, by transfer to the Provider's bank card shown in the Bot or through a payment system connected to the Bot.

5.2. For manual payments the Customer sends the payment receipt to the Bot. The Provider checks the payment within 1 business day and adds the package to the balance.

5.3. The Provider issues an electronic receipt for every payment in accordance with tax law.

5.4. When an analysis starts, 1 analysis (2 for recordings longer than 60 minutes) is reserved from the balance and charged once the analysis succeeds. **If the analysis fails because of a technical error, the reservation is returned automatically.**

5.5. An account's balance is shared by all its branches.

5.6. Money for unused paid analyses is refunded only if the Service was unavailable for more than 3 days through the Provider's fault, in proportion to the analyses left. Otherwise payments are not refundable.

5.7. The history of analyses and payments in the Bot confirms that the Service was provided.

## 6. Rights and obligations

**The Customer:**

- informs clients and employees in advance that conversations are recorded and analysed, and obtains their consent;
- sends only conversations related to the studio's business;
- gives account access only to authorised staff;
- does not use the Service for unlawful purposes.

**The Provider:**

- performs paid analyses as set out in this offer;
- processes data in accordance with the Privacy Policy and never shows it to other studios;
- deletes audio recordings after analysis.

**The Provider's right:** to suspend an account without notice if the terms are breached, including when recordings are sent without consent or are unlawful.

## 7. Liability and force majeure

7.2. The Provider is not liable for decisions the Customer makes based on the analysis results or for their consequences.

7.3. The Customer is liable for all claims related to missing consent of clients and employees.

7.4. Neither party is liable for failure to perform due to force majeure (natural disasters, decisions of public authorities, large-scale outages of the internet or third-party services).
