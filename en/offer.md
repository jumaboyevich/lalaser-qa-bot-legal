## 1. Definitions

- **Provider** — Asraqulov Ilxomjon Jumaboy o'g'li (Ilkhomjon Asraqulov), self-employed individual, TIN: 568780380.
- **Customer** — a legal entity, an individual entrepreneur or their representative (studio manager) who accepts this offer.
- **Bot** — the Telegram bot @lalaser_qa_bot.
- **Service** — AI analysis of recorded conversations and preparation of a report.
- **Analysis** — the billing unit of the Service: the analysis of one audio recording up to 60 minutes long. A recording longer than 60 minutes counts as 2 analyses. The full text of a conversation counts the same as an analysis; the analysis together with the full text counts double (e.g. 4 analyses for a recording longer than 60 minutes).
- **Package** — a prepaid set of a fixed number of analyses.
- **Balance** — the unused analyses left in the Customer's account.
- **Account** — the Customer's account in the Bot. One or more branches are linked to it.
- **Owner** — a user who runs the Account on behalf of the Customer: all branches, the balance, payments and the team.
- **Manager** — a user added to the Account by an Owner's invitation who works in the branches assigned to them.
- **Team** — the Owners and managers of an Account.

## 3. Service and limitations

3.1. The Customer sends an audio recording to the Bot and selects the result (analysis, full text of the conversation or both), the result language and the employee. The Provider analyses the recording against the studio's standards and delivers a score and a written report and/or the full text of the conversation in the Bot. If the Customer chooses, the report is also sent to a linked Telegram group; the full text is sent only to the Customer.

3.2. The analysis is produced automatically by artificial intelligence and is **advisory only**. It may contain inaccuracies. Decisions about employees (rewards, penalties, dismissal) should not rest on the report alone.

3.3. The quality of the analysis depends on the recording: noise, quiet speech or overlapping conversations make the result worse.

3.4. The Provider aims for uninterrupted operation. Temporary interruptions may still occur due to maintenance or outages at third parties (Telegram, the AI provider, hosting).

3.5. Within 7 days after an analysis the Customer may dispute its result with the «Dispute the analysis» button in the Bot. The Provider handles the appeal: listens to the recording, analyses it again free of charge if needed and answers in the Bot. The Provider decides on the appeal, including whether analyses are returned to the balance.

## 5. Payment and balance

5.1. Payment is 100% in advance, through the Rahmat payment service (Payme, Click, Uzum, Paynet or a bank card) or by transfer to the Provider's bank card shown in the Bot.

5.2. When paying through Rahmat, the Customer receives a personal payment link for the chosen package in the Bot. It must be paid within 2 hours; after that a new link has to be requested in the Bot. The package is added to the balance automatically once the payment service confirms the payment. For a card transfer, the Customer sends the payment receipt to the Bot; the Provider checks the payment within 1 business day and adds the package to the balance.

5.3. The Provider issues an electronic receipt for every payment in accordance with tax law. For payments through Rahmat the fiscal receipt is generated by the payment service and its link is sent in the Bot.

5.4. When an analysis starts, 1 analysis (2 for recordings longer than 60 minutes; double for the analysis together with the full text) is reserved from the balance and charged once the analysis succeeds. **If the analysis fails because of a technical error, the reservation is returned automatically.**

5.5. An account's balance is shared by all its branches and team members.

5.6. Money for unused paid analyses is refunded only if the Service was unavailable for more than 3 days through the Provider's fault, in proportion to the analyses left. Otherwise payments are not refundable.

5.7. The history of analyses and payments in the Bot confirms that the Service was provided.

## 6. Team: owners and managers

6.1. An Account may have one or more Owners and managers. An Owner runs all branches, the balance, payments and the team. A manager works only in the branches assigned to them: sends audio, manages employees, sees ratings and reports, and may top up the shared balance.

6.2. An Owner may assign a manager one, several or all branches and change this at any time; a branch may have several managers.

6.3. There is no separate charge for team members. All analyses are taken from the Account's shared balance.

6.4. An Owner adds team members with a one-time invitation link created in the Bot and may remove them at any time. A link is valid for a limited time and can be cancelled until it is used. The Owner gives links only to authorised persons.

6.5. Every team member accepts this offer and the Privacy policy themselves before using the Bot. A manager accepts the offer as the Customer's representative; the Customer remains the party to the contract.

6.6. An Owner may invite another person as an Owner. Once the invitation is accepted, the new Owner gets control of payments, the balance history and the team. The inviting person, at their choice, stays an Owner or becomes the manager of chosen branches.

6.7. If a person who has an Account of their own and is its only Owner accepts an invitation to another Account, their Account joins the inviting one (merge). Branches, employees, the analysis history, linked groups and pending payments move; unused analyses are added to the shared balance and the later of the two expiry dates applies. A merge is confirmed twice in the Bot and cannot be undone. Bonuses received earlier are not granted again.

6.8. If the Account has other Owners too, only the Provider performs the merge, after all Owners have agreed. A user who cannot complete an invitation alone contacts the Provider with the «Ask an admin for help» button in the Bot.

6.9. A person who is a manager in another Account leaves it when accepting an invitation. When a manager is removed or replaced, the history of analyses they sent stays with the branch, and analyses spent are not returned.
