## 2. Data collected

| Category | Data | Whose data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telegram account | Telegram ID, name, username, app language | Manager (user) |
| Settings | Bot language, report language, auto-send, date of accepting the offer and policy | Manager |

2.2. Audio recordings may contain health information (for example skin type, conditions, medication). Such information is used only for the purposes in section 3.

## 3. Purposes and legal basis

Data is processed only to:

- analyse conversations between employees and clients against the studio's standards and prepare reports;
- show the studio's management employee ratings and analysis history;
- run accounts, branches, balances and payments;
- monitor service quality, find errors and keep the Service secure;
- meet legal obligations.

3.1. Legal basis: the user's consent and performance of the contract concluded under the [Public Offer](offer.md).

3.2. The Provider does not sell data, does not share it with third parties for advertising and does not use it to train AI models.

## 4. Audio recordings and studio clients

4.1. For the data of employees and clients in audio recordings, **the studio is the data owner** and the Provider processes it on the studio's instructions.

4.2. The manager (studio) sending audio warrants that:

- the client was informed in advance that the conversation is recorded and analysed for quality control (for example by a notice in the studio or a clause in the client contract) and consented;
- the employee is informed about and agrees to the recording and analysis of their conversations;
- the audio contains only conversations related to the studio's business.

4.3. The Provider does not verify consent. The studio is responsible if consent is missing.

4.4. A studio's data is seen by its team: an Owner sees all branches, a manager only the branches assigned to them. The team list shows members' Telegram names and usernames.

4.5. When a studio's Owner joins their account to another studio (clause 6.7 of the Public offer), they, as the data owner, hand the branches, the employee list and the analysis reports over to that studio; from then on that studio's team sees them.

4.6. When a user asks for help with an invitation, the Provider sees the invitation details (studio, role, branches, the user's account) to resolve the request.

## 5. Sharing with third parties

To provide the Service, data is shared only with the following providers and only as far as necessary:

| Provider | Purpose | Data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telegram | Messaging through the bot | Messages, files |
| Google (Gemini API, paid tier) | AI analysis of audio | Audio recording, analysis rules |
| Railway (cloud hosting) | Hosting the bot and the database | Data in section 2 (except audio) |
| Rahmat (payment service, Uzbekistan) | Accepting online payments and issuing fiscal receipts | Amount and payment link; the payer enters payment details on the Rahmat page themselves |

5.1. An audio file sent for analysis is deleted from Google's servers as soon as the analysis ends. The Provider uses Google's paid API tier, under whose terms Google does not use submitted data to train its models.

5.2. Data may be disclosed to competent public authorities on request where the law requires.

5.3. The user consents to the transfer of data to the foreign providers listed in section 5 (cross-border transfer).

## 6. Retention

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Audio recording | Not stored: deleted from the server and from Google right after the analysis (successful or failed) |
| Reports, scores, employee list | While the account is active; deleted within 30 days after the account is deleted |
| Account, branches, settings | Until the account is deleted |
| Payment and balance history | For the period required by tax law |
| Technical logs | Up to 90 days |

6.1. Reports sent to Telegram chats and groups stay in Telegram. Deleting them is up to the user and the group admins.

## 7. Security

- each studio sees only its own branches, employees and reports, and a manager only the branches assigned to them; access rights are checked on every request;
- only the Provider has access to the database;
- data is transferred over encrypted channels (HTTPS/TLS);
- users are never shown error details, only a request ID;
- audio files are stored temporarily and deleted right after analysis.

8.1. Requests are sent to the contacts in section 9 and handled within 10 business days.

8.2. A studio client or employee should contact the studio first about their data; the Provider helps the studio fulfil the request.
